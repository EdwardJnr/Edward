# Katungo brand rules

- Every piece of Katungo material (video, deck, social post, document, design) uses the official logo.
  - Logo files: `brand/katungo-logo-original.webp` (source) and `brand/katungo-logo-white-wordmark.png` (cropped).
  - The wordmark is white with a mint tagline, so place it on Forest Teal (#0F4C3A) or another dark background, never on white.
  - Videos close on a logo end card.
- Colours: Forest Teal #0F4C3A, Mint Seafoam #2FD79A. Body type: Helvetica (Inter Tight as fallback).
