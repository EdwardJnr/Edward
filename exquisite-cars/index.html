06Automotive art direction
Exquisite Cars — Drive Your Distinction
Every angle
has a purpose.
Sculpted light. Considered composition. A focus on the details that make every vehicle worth discovering.
Hero · profile, top-lit, controlled reflection
Front · light line
Detail · wheel and arch
Rear · tail signature
Light to reveal
Top and edge light define the shape. Never bury the car in black.
Three angles
Three-quarter front for heroes, profile for proportion, close-ups for craft.
Grade
Cool shadows, neutral highlights, lifted blacks. No green cast.
Real stock
Shoot actual inventory in real Harare locations wherever possible.
