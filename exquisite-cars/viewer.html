Description

What if a dealership's identity could say more than the vehicles it sells? This self-initiated concept builds a complete brand experience around the existing Exquisite Cars identity and its tagline, "Excellence Through Passion". The green X and navy wordmark stay exactly as supplied. Around them sits a cinematic visual system of carbon black, midnight navy and a restrained signature green. The campaign platform, Drive Your Distinction, connects each vehicle to the reason someone buys it: presence for the executive, value for the everyday driver, craftsmanship for the enthusiast. One master poster sets the type, grade and grid, and every later piece inherits it: stock adverts, WhatsApp Status, a digital showroom, stationery, signage and the handover itself. Every listing is designed to be read on a phone in two seconds and answered with one tap on WhatsApp. Self-initiated concept project. Not commissioned by or affiliated with Exquisite Cars. Proposed applications are illustrative; names, figures and listings are fictitious. Logo © its owner. Creative direction and design: Edward Katai · Katungo, Harare
