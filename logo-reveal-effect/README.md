# Logo Reveal Studio

A logo reveal effect that works on any logo, made for CapCut.

Open `index.html` (or the published page), drop in a logo, pick an effect and export an MP4. The page reads the logo's shape for itself, so every effect works with any logo.

**Clean & Minimalist:** Draw & Rise, Build & Type, Liquid Wipe, Line Matte, Fade & Scale
**High-Tech & Cinematic:** Glitch, Light Leak, Particle Assembly
**Dynamic & High-Energy:** Kinetic Type (with your own keywords), Shockwave, 3D Flip
**Organic & Texture:** Smoke & Ink, Hologram

You choose the background (white, black, green screen, any colour, or transparent), the format (16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:5), the length (1–5 seconds) and the logo size. On green screen, the effects leave out full-frame flashes and washes so the key stays clean. Export works in Chrome, Edge and Safari on a computer.

## Using it in CapCut

**Logo with no background:** choose Transparent. You then get four exports:
- **Transparent WebM:** full quality with a true alpha channel. Add it as an Overlay in CapCut.
- **Transparent GIF:** 960 px wide at 25 fps, loops, works almost everywhere. GIF transparency is on or off per pixel, so soft edges and glows become hard-edged.
- **Green-screen MP4:** MP4 (H.264) cannot hold transparency in any app, so this is the MP4 route: add it as an Overlay and use Remove BG → Chroma key.
- **PNG sequence (.zip):** for Premiere Pro, After Effects or DaVinci Resolve.

Any background can also be exported as a GIF.

**Logo over footage:** export with Green screen. In CapCut, add the clip as an Overlay above your footage and stretch it to fill the frame. Then open Remove BG → Chroma key, pick the green, and set Intensity to about 20–30 and Shadow to about 10.

**Quick blend:** export with White, put the clip above your footage and set Blend → Multiply. This works best for dark or saturated logos.

**End card or intro:** export with any solid background and use it as a normal clip.

Transparent PNGs give the cleanest result. JPGs on a plain white background also work, because the page removes the white.
