Using it in CapCut

Logo over footage

  1. Export with Green screen.
  2. In CapCut, add it as an Overlay above your footage and stretch it to fill the frame.
  3. Open Remove BG → Chroma key, pick the green, set Intensity about 20–30 and Shadow about 10.

Quick blend on footage

  1. Export with White.
  2. Place it above your footage and set Blend → Multiply. The white drops away.

Works best for dark or saturated logos.

End card or intro

  1. Export with White, Black or a brand colour.
  2. Drop it on the main track like any other clip.

Match the format to your project: 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Reels and TikTok.
