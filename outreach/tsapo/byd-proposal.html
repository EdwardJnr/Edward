From interest to ownership: converting Zimbabwe's EV sceptics

Where BYD stands

The showroom is open, the Atto 8 launched in August, local assembly is agreed and a charging network is planned. Awareness is no longer the problem. The harder question is the one every buyer asks privately: will this work for me, here? They worry about load-shedding, chargers outside Harare, servicing and resale. Launch content cannot answer that. Proof from people like them can, with a sales process that responds the moment they ask.

What we propose

1 · Proof from local owners

Six filmed stories from private owners and fleet operators, covering real running costs, charging routines and servicing. Short films answering the ten questions buyers ask most. AI-produced images of each model in local settings, at volume.

2 · A case for fleets

Fleet and corporate buyers are likely to carry the volume. We build a savings calculator comparing fuel with electricity on a buyer's own routes, plus a fleet pack for procurement teams and boards.

3 · An enquiry-to-sale system

A WhatsApp assistant that answers questions on range, charging and price at any hour, books test drives and passes fleet leads to sales. Every lead is tracked to a sale and reported monthly.
