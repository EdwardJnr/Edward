Proposal for Tsapo Group · BYD Zimbabwe · 22 October 2026

Made here: documenting BYD vehicle assembly in Zimbabwe

The opportunity

Assembling electric vehicles and buses in Zimbabwe is a national first, watched closely by government, fleet buyers, investors and the public. We propose to document it from the start, on film and in photographs, at a standard that holds up beside BYD's own global material, and to turn that record into launch content Tsapo and BYD can use for years.
