The Story-to-Scale Method

  1. Find the right customers. Which customers make the business money, and why they buy.
  2. Sharpen the reason to choose. The promise, proof, offers and voice that make them pick you.
  3. Capture the proof. Real photography, film and testimonials, with AI producing visuals at volume.
  4. Build the system. AI workflows that reach customers at the right moment, handle enquiries and track every lead to a sale.
  5. Convert and keep. Run it monthly. Measure enquiries, sales and repeat customers, and adjust.

Once a year the cycle returns to stage one, because the right customers shift as a business grows.
